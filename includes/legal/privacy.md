# PRISM Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026

PRISM (“PRISM,” “the System,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is a web-based system for IERB progress monitoring, research document management, notifications, and reporting. PRISM is operated by Research Progress Monitoring Section for authorized users associated with Centro Escolar University - Malolos

This Privacy Policy explains the information PRISM collects, why it is used, how it is protected, and how Google user data is handled.

## 1. Information PRISM Processes

PRISM may process information necessary to operate institutional research-management workflows, including:

- name and institutional account information;
- institutional email address;
- Student ID or Employee ID;
- academic unit, program/course, year level, and academic year where applicable;
- Research/Capstone Adviser assignment and research-group information;
- protocol codes and IERB progress information;
- uploaded research and IERB-related documents;
- document review decisions, remarks, versions, and submission history;
- notifications and workflow communications;
- account, security, activity, and audit records; and
- reports generated from authorized PRISM records.

PRISM processes this information only for legitimate system, academic, research-administration, security, reporting, and institutional purposes.

## 2. Gmail API and Google User Data

PRISM uses the Google Gmail API solely to send PRISM-generated email through a designated institutional or system Gmail/Google Workspace sender account.

PRISM requests only:

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send

This permission allows PRISM to send email from the authorized sender account.

PRISM does not use the Gmail API to read, search, retrieve, modify, organize, or delete the sender's existing email messages, inbox contents, contacts, or other Gmail data.

PRISM uses Gmail authorization only for sending legitimate system communications such as account-related messages, password-reset communications, submission and review notifications, deadlines, follow-ups, and other authorized PRISM workflow messages.

Google OAuth credentials and refresh tokens are stored in protected server-side configuration and are not made available to ordinary PRISM users.

PRISM does not sell Google user data, use Google user data for advertising, or use Gmail API data to train general-purpose or personalized artificial-intelligence models.

PRISM's use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

## 3. How Information Is Used

PRISM uses information to:

- authenticate and manage authorized accounts;
- manage Student, Adviser, and RPMS workflows;
- process and track research and IERB documents;
- provide document-review and progress-monitoring features;
- send authorized notifications and communications;
- generate institutional research-management reports;
- maintain security and audit records;
- investigate errors, abuse, or unauthorized access; and
- maintain the availability and integrity of the System.

PRISM does not use personal information for unrelated advertising or commercial profiling.

## 4. AI-Assisted Reporting

PRISM may provide AI-assisted aggregate reporting features for authorized RPMS personnel.

Where external AI services are used, PRISM is designed to limit the data transmitted to what is required for the authorized reporting function and to apply applicable privacy protections.

Google Gmail API data and OAuth credentials are not provided to PRISM's AI service providers and are not used for AI model training.

AI-generated reports are intended to assist authorized users and should be reviewed by a human before being relied upon for official decisions.

## 5. Information Sharing

PRISM information may be accessible to authorized users according to their assigned role and legitimate institutional responsibilities.

PRISM does not sell personal information.

Information may be disclosed where necessary:

- to provide PRISM's authorized functions;
- to infrastructure or service providers acting on behalf of the operator;
- to comply with applicable law, legal process, or institutional obligations;
- to protect the security and integrity of PRISM and its users; or
- with appropriate authorization or consent.

Access is subject to PRISM's role-based authorization controls.

## 6. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as necessary for legitimate institutional, academic, research-management, audit, security, and legal purposes.

PRISM supports account archiving and controlled account deletion. Some records may need to be retained longer where an account is subject to a retention hold, unresolved workflow, audit requirement, legal obligation, or other legitimate institutional requirement.

Deletion of an account does not necessarily require deletion of records that must lawfully or institutionally be preserved independently.

## 7. Security

PRISM uses technical and organizational safeguards intended to protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, loss, or destruction.

These safeguards include role-based access controls, authentication, authorization checks, session protections, protected server storage, audit records, and controlled access to sensitive configuration such as OAuth credentials.

No internet-connected system can guarantee absolute security.
